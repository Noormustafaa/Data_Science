Decision Tree classification works like a flowchart or a series of Yes/No questions to make a decision. You start at the top, answer questions step-by-step based on your data, and follow the branches until you land on a final answer (a leaf node).

Asaan Example: Cricket Khelna Hai Ya Nahi?

Instead of calculating complex percentages like Naive Bayes, a Decision Tree makes a clear rulebook based on your past data:


               Is it raining?
              /              \
         (Yes)                (No)
         /                      \
  Don't Play               Is humidity high?
                           /               \
                      (Yes)                 (No)
                      /                       \
               Don't Play                     Play!


Steps:

Start with the Data

Choose the best feature

Make branches

Repeat the process for each branch
